Adding numbers vertically by regrouping

The numbers with a sum of ones which exceed 9 can be added vertically.

Example

Add vertically.

77
+ 19
Solution
77
+ 19
96

Steps

1. Add the ones: 7 + 9 = 16. Write 6 in the ones place of the answer. Take 1 ten to the tens place.

1
77
+ 19
6

2. Add the tens: 1 + 7 + 1 = 9. Write 9 in the tens place of the answer.

Therefore, the answer is 96.

77
+ 19
96
